--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE UNDERLYING TRACE  (this is what "envelope" means)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every trial contributes one 1-D trace, stored in the wide CSV as `dir_val_0 ...
dir_val_6226` and produced by the upstream pipeline as the column
`envelope_of_rms`. It is built as:

  1. d_px       : per-frame Euclidean eye->proboscis distance in pixels, from the
                  class-2 DeepLabCut/tracking output, valid range 10-250 px.
  2. multiplier : a continuous 1.0-2.0 weight derived from the centred proboscis
                  *angle* percentage, m = 1 + ln(1 + (angle%/100)(e-1)) for
                  positive angles, 1.0 otherwise. Extensions directed toward the
                  odour port are therefore up-weighted up to 2x.
  3. d_px_weighted = d_px * multiplier.
  4. combined_pct = 100 * (d_px_weighted - gmin_fly) / (max(gmax_fly, 150 px)
                    - gmin_fly), i.e. min-max normalisation to 0-100 using
                    per-fly (per fly-number) weighted bounds, with a 150 px floor
                    on the upper bound.
  5. rolling RMS over a 0.25 s centred window (10 frames at 40 fps).
  6. Hilbert analytic-signal magnitude of that RMS, smoothed by the same 0.25 s
     centred rolling mean  ->  `envelope_of_rms`.

CONSEQUENCE FOR THE TABLE: the envelope is in *normalised units, 0-100 % of the
fly's angle-weighted extension range* -- NOT pixels. Empirically the dir_val_*
matrix spans exactly [0, 100] (median 9.96). Any caption or definition that says
"extension in px" for local_*/global-style envelope features is wrong.

The ONLY features carrying true pixel units are `global_max` and
`trimmed_global_min` (see section 4), which are per-fly raw-distance statistics.

Recording length: traces are 40 fps and ~6000 frames (~150 s); the wide matrix is
padded to 6227 columns with trailing NaN, and `interpolate_nans` holds the last
finite value across that padding.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. EPOCH DEFINITIONS  (two families use slightly different bounds -- verified)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SIGNAL family (notebook 04 and xgb_ordinal.py):
    ODOR_ON_SEC = 32, ODOR_OFF_SEC = 62, FPS = 40
    before : frames 0-1280      =  0-32 s
    during : frames 1280-2480   = 32-62 s   (30 s odorant presentation)
    after  : frames 2480-end    = 62 s to the end of the recording (~150 s)
  The "after" window is therefore the whole post-odour remainder (~88 s), not a
  30 s block. Only `persistence` uses it.

ENGINEERED family (upstream envelope_combined.py, legacy frame constants):
    BEFORE_FRAMES = 1260, DURING_FRAMES = 1200, AFTER_FRAMES = 1200
    before : frames 0-1260      =  0.0-31.5 s
    during : frames 1260-2460   = 31.5-61.5 s
    after  : frames 2460-3660   = 61.5-91.5 s
  So the engineered AUC ratios use a bounded 30 s post-odour window, offset by
  ~0.5 s from the signal-family bounds.

CONSEQUENCE FOR THE TABLE: the caption "three 30 s trial periods: before
(0-30 s), during (30-60 s), after (60-90 s)" is wrong on all three counts.
Suggested caption wording is given in section 5.

(The newer pipeline can override these per trial from rig sidecars via
`trial_odor_on_s` / `trial_odor_off_s` / `trial_duration_s`; those columns are
absent from the training CSV, so the constants above are what the trained model
actually saw.)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. POST-PROCESSING APPLIED TO THE FEATURE MATRIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
After assembly (`preprocess_features`): +/-inf -> NaN, then every column is
median-imputed with the column median of the batch being scored. This matters
for the two AUC ratios, which are NaN whenever the pre-odour supra-threshold area
is zero: only 2783 / 4394 rows have a finite AUC-During-Before-Ratio, so ~37 % of
rows receive the median for those two features.

4. PER-FEATURE VERIFIED DEFINITIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

--- ENGINEERED / ENVELOPE FAMILY (11) ------------------------------------------

global_max
  Per-FLY maximum eye->proboscis distance in PIXELS, read from the cached
  class-2 distance-statistics sidecar for that fly/slot (falls back to the
  observed envelope span when the sidecar is missing). Constant across all trials
  of a fly; it is a rig/animal-scale covariate, not a trial-level response.
  Observed range 39.9-250.0 px.
  [DRAFT SAID: "Maximum of the extension in pxs over the whole trial" -- wrong on
   both "whole trial" (it is per fly) and on it being a trial-level quantity.]

trimmed_global_min
  Per-FLY retracted-baseline estimate in PIXELS: the 0.01th percentile of all
  raw class-2 eye->proboscis distances pooled over that fly's trials (fallback:
  1st percentile of the envelope samples). Also constant within a fly.
  Observed range 18.2-100.9 px.
  [DRAFT was broadly right but implied per-trial; state that it is per fly and
   that the trimming is percentile-based on the raw pixel distances.]

local_min
  Minimum of the envelope over the WHOLE trial (all finite frames), normalised
  units.
  [DRAFT SAID "within the stimulus epoch" -- WRONG. It is whole-trial.]

local_max
  Maximum of the envelope over the WHOLE trial (all finite frames), normalised
  units.
  [DRAFT SAID "within the stimulus epoch" -- WRONG. It is whole-trial.]

local_min_before
  Minimum of the envelope within the pre-odour epoch (frames 0-1260). Correct in
  draft.

local_max_before
  Maximum of the envelope within the pre-odour epoch (frames 0-1260). Correct in
  draft.

local_min_during
  Minimum of the envelope within the odour epoch (frames 1260-2460). Correct in
  draft.

local_max_over_global_min
  local_max (WHOLE-TRIAL envelope maximum, normalised units) divided by the
  fly-level trimmed minimum (pixels). A dimensionless extension index; note it
  deliberately mixes the normalised envelope numerator with the pixel-scale
  denominator, so it is a scale-corrected ratio rather than a physical ratio.
  [DRAFT SAID "stimulus-epoch maximum" -- WRONG, it is the whole-trial maximum.]

local_max_during_over_global_min
  local_max_during (maximum of the envelope within the odour epoch) divided by
  the same fly-level trimmed minimum. Same mixed-unit caveat.
  [Draft correct apart from the unit caveat. This is the 2nd most important
   feature by permutation importance.]

AUC-During-Before-Ratio
  Ratio of the SUPRA-THRESHOLD envelope area during odour to that before odour.
  The area is sum(max(env - threshold, 0)) * dt over the epoch, where
  threshold = (fly-level median of all pre-odour envelope samples) + 3 * sigma,
  and sigma = 1.4826 * MAD of THIS trial's pre-odour window. Undefined (NaN,
  later median-imputed) whenever the pre-odour area is zero.
  [DRAFT SAID "area under the curve" -- must say supra-threshold area, and the
   threshold definition matters.]

AUC-After-Before-Ratio
  Same construction for the post-odour epoch (frames 2460-3660, 61.5-91.5 s)
  over the pre-odour epoch.

--- SIGNAL FAMILY (13) ---------------------------------------------------------
All computed from the same envelope trace, on the 32 / 62 s epoch bounds, after
linear interpolation of NaN gaps. eps = 1e-10 guards every denominator.

mean_shift_z
  (mean of during - mean of before) / SD of before. Correct in draft.

std_ratio
  SD of during / SD of before. Correct in draft.

peak_z
  (max of during - mean of before) / SD of before. Correct in draft.

auc_ratio
  Ratio of the MEAN-NORMALISED trapezoidal area of the during epoch to that of
  the before epoch, i.e. trapz(seg)/len(seg) for each epoch. Because of the
  length normalisation this is effectively a ratio of mean envelope levels, and
  it is NOT thresholded (unlike AUC-During-Before-Ratio above).
  [Draft's "ratio of during-odour to pre-odour trace area" is misleading without
   the per-frame normalisation; also worth distinguishing from the engineered
   AUC ratio, which is a different quantity with the same name.]

total_power_ratio
  Sum of the five band powers below for the during epoch divided by the same sum
  for the before epoch -- i.e. the 0-10 Hz total-power ratio. It is the sum of
  the band powers, not an independently integrated broadband power.

power_ratio_vlow / low / mid / high / vhigh
  During/before ratio of Welch power summed over the half-open bands
  [0, 0.5), [0.5, 1), [1, 3), [3, 5), [5, 10) Hz.
  Welch parameters: each epoch is mean-subtracted first; nperseg = min(256, n),
  50 % overlap, fs = 40 Hz (so ~0.156 Hz resolution). Band powers are plain sums
  of PSD bins in the band. Draft bands are correct.

frac_above_baseline
  Fraction of odour-epoch frames exceeding (mean of before + 2 * SD of before).
  Correct in draft.

persistence
  (mean of after - mean of before) / (mean of during - mean of before).
  A dimensionless RETENTION ratio: what fraction of the odour-evoked mean shift
  is still present after odour offset. It is unbounded and can be negative, and
  "after" here is 62 s to the end of the recording.
  [DRAFT SAID "Duration for which extension remained above threshold once
   exceeded" -- COMPLETELY WRONG. This is the single most important correction
   in the table; it is a ratio, not a duration, and no threshold is involved.]

time_to_peak_frac
  argmax of the during epoch divided by the number of during-epoch frames, i.e.
  latency to peak as a fraction of the odour epoch. Correct in draft.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. SUGGESTED REPLACEMENT CAPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Feature set supplied to the trial-level PER classifier. All features are
  derived from a single per-trial trace: the Hilbert envelope of a 0.25 s
  rolling RMS of the angle-weighted eye-to-proboscis distance, min-max
  normalised per fly to 0-100 (arbitrary units, a.u.). Signal-family epochs are
  before (pre-odour baseline, 0-32 s), during (odorant presentation, 32-62 s)
  and after (post-odour, 62 s to the end of the ~150 s recording);
  envelope-family epochs use the pipeline frame constants and are 0-31.5 s,
  31.5-61.5 s and 61.5-91.5 s. Spectral features are ratios of during-epoch to
  before-epoch Welch power (mean-subtracted segments, nperseg = min(256, n),
  50 % overlap). global_max and trimmed_global_min are per-fly statistics of the
  raw pixel distance and are constant across a fly's trials. Feature families
  correspond to the engineered-only and signal-only arms of the ablation in
  Table ref{tab:classifier-performance}.

7. SUMMARY OF CORRECTIONS TO THE DRAFT TABLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. persistence is a retention RATIO, not a duration above threshold. (critical)
 2. Epochs are 0-32 / 32-62 / 62-end s (signal) and 0-31.5 / 31.5-61.5 /
    61.5-91.5 s (envelope), not three clean 30 s blocks at 0/30/60/90.
 3. local_min and local_max are whole-trial, not stimulus-epoch.
 4. local_max_over_global_min uses the whole-trial maximum, not the
    stimulus-epoch maximum.
 5. Envelope features are in normalised a.u. (0-100), not pixels; only
    global_max and trimmed_global_min are in pixels.
 6. global_max and trimmed_global_min are per-FLY constants, not per-trial.
 7. The engineered AUC ratios are supra-threshold areas with a specific
    fly-median + 3*MAD threshold; the signal-family auc_ratio is a different,
    unthresholded, length-normalised quantity.
 8. total_power_ratio is the sum of the five band powers, i.e. 0-10 Hz.
 9. The feature list itself (24 features, 11 + 13) is confirmed correct against
    the deployed booster.
